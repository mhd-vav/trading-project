# Bundle 4 — Sentiment / Positioning / Crowd

- **Shared frame:** COT (commitment of traders) reports, funding rates, long/short
  ratios, social sentiment, options skew.
- **The single question the bundle answers:** *Is sentiment confirming the move, or is it
  a contrarian warning?*
- **Member agents (with the built-in opposition):**
  - **Confirmation** agent — argues crowd positioning confirms the trend.
  - **Contrarian** agent — argues extreme positioning is a reversal warning.
  - **Positioning referee** — quantifies how extreme the positioning is historically.
  - **Derivatives-skew referee** — reads options skew / risk reversal for tail hints.
- **Why the internal debate is logical:** "the crowd is right, trade with them" vs "the
  crowd is crowded, fade them" is the classic sentiment tension. The referees decide
  *whether positioning is extreme enough* to favor the contrarian view — without that,
  the debate is just opinion.
